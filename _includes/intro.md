## About Me

I am a final-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Amsterdam ([INDElab](https://indelab.org/)), advised by [Prof. Dr. Paul Groth](https://pgroth.com) and [Dr. Klim Zaporojets](https://klimzaporojets.github.io/). My defense is planned for March 2027.

I work on machine learning for knowledge graphs, where the data keeps changing and many entities look alike. I build models that add signals which are usually left out, such as time and how the graph changes, so that entity linking, link prediction and recommendation stay accurate on sparse, long-tail and look-alike cases. The same problem appears in industry as entity resolution under data drift.

**Research interests:** multi-modal knowledge graphs, entity linking and resolution, link prediction, recommendation, large language models.
